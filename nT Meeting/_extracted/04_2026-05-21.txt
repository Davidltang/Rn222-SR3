1'. Plan for next TPC commissioning schedules (AC + experts)
During the recent TPC commissioning activities, we are encountering some diﬃculties for data
taking (DAQ crashes, board failure,…), and DAQ team is working very hard to solve the situation
(See Andrew's report).
After the discussion with relevant experts, we suggest to proceed with two steps.
Minimal condition for the commissioning test: all boards working and continuous data1.
taking (ex. even with few seconds of deadtime due to the crash etc)
Long-term solution for science run data taking: stable and minimizing the deadtime etc2.
To achieve the Step1, we suggest to give a priority to DAQ team until the end of next week,
since the investigation requires various condition of PMT and electrodes to have lower and
higher DAQ rates.
In case the DAQ still do not show reliable performances by the end of that period, we will revert
the DAQ to the SR2 version.
