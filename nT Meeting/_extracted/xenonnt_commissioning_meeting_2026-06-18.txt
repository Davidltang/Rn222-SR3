The plan agered in the NV group is to increase the concentration by a factor of 5 with respect to
the SR2 (and current) value, reaching 2500 ppm (compared to the current 500 ppm).
At this concentration, from MC simulations we expect to achieve
a neutron tagging eﬃciency of about 83%,
with an average capture time of about 30 μs.
This approach allows us to keep roughly half of the available Gd salt in reserve as a
precautionary measure, with the possibility of adding it at a later stage —for instance during the
usual yearly maintenance of the cryogenic plants—if everything proceeds smoothly.
The foreseen performance at 5000 ppm is only marginally better than at 2500 ppm:
neutron tagging eﬃciency: 85%
average neutron capture time: 20 μs
The salt insertion can start next Monday, June 22. The operation should take about 10 days to
complete, followed by approximately 10 additional days to achieve a uniform concentration
throughout the WT.
In this way, by mid-July we should have the NV ready in the SR3 conﬁguration.
