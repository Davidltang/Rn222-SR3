Slow Control (Mahmoud)
SCADA crash on 28-May: the incident was in investigated. In short: one of the processes that
SCADA uses was shut down by Windows, this lead to the failure of other dependent processes
on SCADA leading to the crash, SCADA2 (redundancy) caught up smoothly after that. It wasn't
a hardware failure . For more technical explanation .
DAQ (Andrew)
The original issue we set out to resolve is now largely solved. We are no longer seeing
corrupted packets!
Unfortunately this issue masked an issue with a selection of boards associated with one of the
VME crates. New power supply ordered and we hope to install tomorrow or Monday. We have
removed boards from one VME crate and disabled all high energy boards to lower load on the
crate. Please use the tpc_bkg_low_energy mode for now. This has all PMTs.
Separate issue identiﬁed with the LED mode where one (apparently) random board is not
triggering.
Wanting to do an electrodes test, but limited currently by availability of people
We should also think about checking the total power consumption for the DAQ from the super
UPS
