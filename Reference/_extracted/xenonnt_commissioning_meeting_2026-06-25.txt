XENON1TWiki - https://xe1t-wiki.lngs.infn.it/On Monday (22/6/2026), users reported that the SCADA WebSpace screens on the webserver turned
black or showed stale/no data. SCADA itself was working ﬁne the entire time, and so were the
Historian and the local Viewer, so this was not a SCADA failure but a problem with the SCADA
WebSpace interface on the webserver. The issue happened shortly after a failed attempt to run the
calibration sequences screen, which crashed in an unusual way, possibly due to an expired DAQ API
key on the HYL account. The causality is not fully established, but the failed script may have
triggered or exposed a service-level issue that aﬀected communication/authentication/permissions
between the SCADA server and the WebSpace server, possibly connected to domain-based access
between the two machines. In practice, this caused the webserver to intermittently stop receiving or
updating project-level values properly. A quick service restart solved it temporarily, but the issue
later returned. Around 13:50 the following day, we performed a quick SCADA restart, which took
about 2 minutes, and this seems to have resolved the issue fully for now.
Alarm notiﬁcation update:
The new alarm notiﬁcation code is deployed since 23/06/2026 at 11:46.
In short: alarms repeating again with the same severity and title within a time window, currently
set to 10 minutes, are suppressed from notiﬁcations. A “ﬂood warning” is notiﬁed to shifters
and on the slack-notiﬁcation channel. All alarms are still sent to the slack-uncensored channel
(more details here) .
Since deployed:
124 alarms were sent to all notiﬁcation channels: email, SMS, and Slack.
84 alarms were suppressed from all notiﬁcation channels: email, SMS, and Slack.
51 “ﬂood warning” messages were generated, each reporting 1 suppressed repeated
alarm.
