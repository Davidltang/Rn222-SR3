LNGS Update (Petr)
LXe PUR:  The Q5 ﬁlter was changed to ST707 one on Monday May 11 (Riccardo, Mattia, JMD
remotely). PM measured electron lifetime after the ﬁlter change was dropped from 1.14 to 0.95
ms (May 11, 21:58). Lifetime is slowly increasing with rate of about 0.08 ms/day reaching 1.10
ms now.

TPC:  Electrodes HV test was started on Monday May 4. Cathode voltage was successfully tested
up to -20.0 kV. Data were taken for diﬀerent voltages including the TPC mode (Cathode at -15.0
kV; Anode at +3.0 kV and Gate at -2.0 kV). Lifetime was measured (with Q5 ﬁlter) to be ~1.4
ms. Data are analyzing now (fast-response team). Test is currently ongoing for the top stack.

muVeto/nVeto:  Veto PMTs are ON 24/7 since a few weeks, with stable data taking. Next AmBe
calibration (center of NV) was successfully completed today. Data analysis is ongoing.

compressor; water leak). The water-cooling system repair is ongoing (Riccardo and Mattia).

Level control commissioning:
As designed
FV-113 open, FV-112 open, LCV-110 is controlled to keep level in accumulator
constant
Speciﬁcally, heat input generates GXe: LCV-110 closed too much → LXe
pushed out, LCV-110 open too much → LXe pushed in
Unfortunately the ﬂow coeﬃcient of the control valve is too large compared to
the very small heat input and the “large” pressure diﬀerence (1.3 bard)
Even at minimal opening, too much LXe ﬂows in
Current implementation to allow some electrodes testing:
Keep accumulator at detector pressure (≈2 bara) instead of LXe pump inlet
pressure (≈3.3 bara)
FV-111 closed, FV-112 closed
Open FV-111 brieﬂy (1s) to admit LXe in every 6 hours of so
Small disturbance in bell LXe level (≈0.1 mm drop)
In parallel, contacted suppliers to have a control valve with enough rangeability
Should know lead time early next week
Alternative implementation, which might reduce the bell LXe level impact
Keep accumulator pressure slightly lower then LXe pump inlet pressure
FV-111 closed, FV-112 open, LCV-110 closed most of the time
