MV & NV
Gadolinium Insertion Operations (Filippo) : Operations started on Monday 25/05 afternoon
and lasted until today 28/05 at 12:00 LNGS time.
A total of about 350 kg of gadolinium salt was inserted into the water tank (WT).
The solution was injected into the WT through the pipe at the bottom of the muon veto (MV).
During nighttime, the Gd-Water circulation loop was activated in automatic mode to help mixing
inside the WT. This loop will continue to operate in the coming days to ensure uniform
gadolinium diﬀusion in the WT volume.
See a detailed log of operations here:  Gadolinium Loading Operations – Daily Log .
Each day we performed a conductivity measurement using samples extracted from three points
of the WT:
Low (L) at the bottom of the MV.
Medium (M) at the bottom of the NV.
High (H): at the top of the WT.
These measurement will continue daily, each week a conductivity measurement using the
ICP-MS will be performed.
The results will be posted on the following table: ⚡ CONDUCTIVITY DATA IN THE WATER
TANK - GADOLINIUM MONITORING .

Slow Control
Incident on 28/5/2026, starting at 13:36:38:
Earlier today SCADA1 appears to have temporarily frozen/​crashed,​ causing an automatic failover to
the redundant SCADA2 server and later a switch back once SCADA1 recovered (14:46). At the
moment there is no indication that detector/​PAC operation itself was compromised. The main issue
was alarm ﬂooding caused by repeated/​stale alarms during the redundancy transition. The positive
side is that the redundancy switch itself appears to have worked correctly. Without the stale/“​always-
on”​ alarms, the transition would likely have been almost completely transparent operationally.
Short term plan (today): Some remaining issues still require a SCADA restart and/or EGD restart
A SCADA restart will retrigger the stale alarms again (temporarily disabling notiﬁcations on the
DB side can minimize the pain to shifters)
Another option is to ﬁrst try an EGD restart, and only do a SCADA restart if still needed
Medium term (next week):
It is high priority to get rid of these stale alarms by disabling/​ﬁxing them on the PAC side That means
updating PAC code and rebuilding EGD PAC-by-PAC I will prepare a list and discuss it on the Slack
channel, including which alarms should remain as is, inhibited, or reduced in severity.
